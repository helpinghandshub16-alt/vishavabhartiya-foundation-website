About us

A legacy that began at a neighbourhood clinic

In 1998 our founder, optometrist Dr. Dhananjay K. Yerne, opened a public hospital and mother-and-child health centre on Kharbi Road, Nagpur, and began free eye check-up camps. Through his earlier organisation, Vishvabhartiya Manav Kalyan Sewa Sanstha, the work reached slums, labour colonies, villages, schools, anganwadis, places of worship of every faith, the Nagpur Central Jail and highway petrol pumps.

On 12 March 2026 this work gained an institutional home: Vishavabhartiya Foundation, a Section 8 not-for-profit company registered with the Ministry of Corporate Affairs.

  • Free eye check-ups and spectacle distribution for people in need
  • Awareness in the local language, where people live and work
  • Registered, CSR-eligible and committed to transparent reporting
