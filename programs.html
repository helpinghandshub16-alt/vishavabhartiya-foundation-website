Health & Eye Care

Free eye check-ups — a legacy since 1998

The longest-running work of our founder and the earlier Sanstha, and now the Foundation's core programme. Free screening is held at the founder's eye-care centre in Nagpur and at camps wherever people gather, with spectacles for those who cannot afford them.

  • Free eye check-up camps and spectacle distribution in slums, labour colonies and villages
  • Camps at schools, colleges, anganwadis, gram panchayats and community halls
  • Camps at temples, Buddha viharas, gurudwaras, mosques and churches, open to all
  • Eye care for inmates at Nagpur Central Jail
  • Screening for truck, taxi and bus drivers at highway petrol pumps
  • General health check-up and blood donation camps
  • Pulse Polio volunteering with Nagpur Municipal Corporation, 1998–2023 (legacy)
  • International Yoga Day 2026 (Foundation programme)

Education & Skill Development

Building confidence in young people

Work with schools and colleges so that students learn about health, science and citizenship, and get a chance to be recognised.

  • Essay and drawing competitions with prizes and certificates (legacy)
  • Personality development camp with UNESCO Club, Nagpur (2004)
  • Science and environment fair with Marathi Vigyan Parishad (2005)
  • Awareness sessions in schools on health, hygiene and the environment
  • Felicitation of students and young volunteers

Women Empowerment

Healthy mothers, confident women

The founder's work began with a mother-and-child health care centre in 1998. The Foundation continues women's health and empowerment programmes and plans livelihood support.

  • Maharashtra Public Hospital & Mother and Child Health Care Centre (opened 1 May 1998)
  • Women's health check-up and awareness camps
  • Women empowerment programmes with local groups
  • Women volunteers leading community programmes

Environment & Plantation

If the Earth is not saved, nothing will remain

Our MD & CEO's Earth Day message sums up the programme: small steps, taken together, every day.

  • Tree plantation drives
  • Cleanliness drives under Swachhata Abhiyan on 2 October
  • Earth Day programme, 22 April 2026 (Foundation programme)
  • Pledges to reduce single-use plastic

Social Awareness & Community Development

Awareness in the language people speak

Talks at temples, schools, farms, bus stands and under village trees, using hoardings, competitions and local language.

  • HIV/AIDS awareness with government programmes (2004)
  • Leprosy Elimination Action Programme (LEAP) in Bhandara district villages, promoted by ALERT India and supported by ANESVAD Foundation
  • Tobacco awareness campaigns
  • Voter enrolment awareness camps in East Nagpur
  • Campaign for city bus services to outskirts and labour colonies
  • Gandhi Jayanti and Lal Bahadur Shastri Jayanti observed with free eye camps
  • Public-awareness articles on eye health in the Navbharat newspaper
