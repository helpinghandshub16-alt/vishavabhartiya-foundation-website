Free Eye Check-Up & Spectacles Distribution Campaign 2026–27

A one-year crowdfunding campaign by Vishavabhartiya Foundation: 144 free eye camps and 14,400 pairs of spectacles for people with weak eyesight who cannot afford care.

Executive summary

Clear sight for 14,400 people in one year

Weak eyesight keeps children from reading the blackboard, workers from earning and elders from living independently — and a simple pair of spectacles is often all it takes. Building on the founder's free eye-care work since 1998, Vishavabhartiya Foundation will run 144 free eye camps over one year (2026–27) and distribute high-quality spectacles to 14,400 beneficiaries.

Status: proposed campaign, open for donations and CSR support. Progress (camps held, people screened, spectacles given) will be published here and in our annual report.
