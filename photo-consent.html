Photo & Media Consent Policy

How we take and use photographs and videos responsibly.

Our approach

Photographs help us report honestly to donors and partners. We take them respectfully and only use them in ways that protect the dignity of the people shown.

Consent

  • We ask for verbal or written consent before close-up photographs of individuals.
  • For children, consent is taken from a parent, guardian or the responsible institution.
  • People may decline to be photographed without any effect on the services they receive.

What we avoid

  • Images during medical examination that expose a person’s body or condition in an undignified way.
  • Publishing names, addresses or health details of beneficiaries alongside their photos.
  • Presenting a partner’s photographs as our own work.

Removal requests

If you appear in a photograph on this website and want it removed, write to us; we will remove it promptly. vishavabhartiya.foundation@gmail.com · +91 94236 32405
